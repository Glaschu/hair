Booking service

How online booking works

You send a client a link, they pick a time, and it lands in your diary. Setting it up takes about ten minutes, once. Ready to start? Open your setup page.

Set it up, once

  1. Turn on online booking

    In Settings, switch on "Online booking". The rest of the booking settings appear underneath.

  2. Open your setup page

    On a computer or another screen, go to your booking site and add /setup to the address. It shows a code.

  3. Link this device

    Tap "Set up booking service", then "Scan a code", and point the camera at the code. If the code is on this same device, use "Copy code" on the page and paste it in the app instead.

  4. Sign in and get approved

    Enter an email and a password — the first time, this makes your account. The app then shows this device's ID. Send it with "Share this ID" to whoever looks after your booking service; once they have approved it, tap "Check again".

    Only if the app asks you to sign in. Some booking services skip this step.

  5. Check your cancellation policy

    Open "Cancellation policy" and read the wording. Clients agree to it before they book, so make it say what you mean.

Day to day

  • Send a client a link

    Open the client's card and tap "Send link". Pick the service and the times you want to offer, then send it by message. The link is theirs alone and works once.

  • Bring bookings into your diary

    Pull down on Today. New bookings arrive with the client's name and number, and anything a client cancelled online is marked cancelled.

  • Block your days off

    In Settings, open "Days off" and pick the dates. Those days are never offered, and can't be booked even from a link you already sent.

  • Cancel a booking

    Open the appointment and tap "Cancel". Your booking page is told too, so the time is free to offer again. If you have no signal, it is sent as soon as you are back.

Good to know

  • Adding your iPad

    On the phone that already works, open "Booking service" and tap "Link another device". Scan that code with the iPad. Don't use the setup page for a second device.

  • Taking deposits

    Deposits need a Stripe account connected to your booking service. Until that is done, bookings work without them. When it is, clients pay when they book, and cancelling asks you how much to refund.

  • What stays private

    A client's name and number are locked on their phone before they are sent, and only your devices can open them. Your booking service never sees who booked.

These steps are also in the app: Settings → Online booking → How it works.
